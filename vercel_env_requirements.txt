When deploying to Vercel, you must add the following Environment Variables in your Vercel Project Settings:

1. NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL
2. NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY
3. SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY
4. RESEND_API_KEY
5. UNSPLASH_ACCESS_KEY

You can copy the exact values from your local `.env.local` file. Make sure not to skip any, as they are required for the database, authentication, and email services to work correctly in production.
